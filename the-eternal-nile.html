Your Itinerary

Your passage through Egypt's sacred heart

You land in Cairo and settle into your hotel. As evening falls, you gather with your guides and fellow travelers for the first time — the companions who will walk this journey with you.

Day 1–3
Stop one

Cairo

Three grounding days in Cairo to begin. Private access to sites most travelers only experience in a crowd — you'll stand before the Sphinx at dawn, descend into the Great Pyramid's inner chambers, and walk through the newly opened Grand Egyptian Museum.

Visiting
  • The Grand Egyptian Museum
  • Private Access to the Sphinx
  • Private Access to the Great Pyramid of Giza
Stop two

Saqqara

A day at Egypt's oldest sacred complex — the necropolis where the stone language of the pharaohs was first written. Imhotep's masterwork, the Step Pyramid of Zoser, and the deep chambers of the Serapeum.

Visiting
  • Pyramid of Zoser
  • The Serapeum
  • Pyramid of Unas
Day 4
Day 5–8
Stop three

Aswan & the Nile

Four days aboard our private dahabeya, sailing from Aswan northward. This is the heart of the journey — sacred ceremonies on the water, temples only accessible by boat, and the stillness the river has always offered seekers.

Visiting
  • Philae (Isis) Temple
  • Private Kom Ombo Temple
  • Sacred ceremonies on board while sailing the Nile
Stop four

Luxor

The journey closes at Al-Moudira — a sanctuary for integration, held between Karnak and the Valley of the Kings. Time to rest, reflect, and let the work of the previous days settle into the body.

Visiting
  • Valley of the Kings
  • Karnak Temple
  • Private dinners
  • Integration Session at Al-Moudira Oasis
