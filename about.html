The three pillars
of transformation

The Modern Ashram is built on three foundations: skilled facilitation, cultural immersion, and spiritual practice. Our journeys are held within safe, intentional spaces — rooted in the wisdom of place and culture, and anchored by ancient practices thoughtfully adapted for modern life. Together, these three pillars create experiences that catalyze deep healing, embodied presence, and lasting inner expansion.

What guides us

Our Mission

At The Modern Ashram, we exist to bring ancient wisdom back into modern life —

We live in a world that moves too fast, demands much, and often pulls us far from who we truly are. Somewhere along the way, many of us lose the plot — to our bodies, our truth, our sense of inner peace. Our mission is to help you find it again.

Our work is rooted in a simple truth: healing is your natural state. Through sacred journeys, natural medicine, and ancient practices, we create the conditions for you to return to yourself — to your wholeness, your clarity, and your own inner knowing…

We do this in one of the most energetically potent places on earth: Ancient Egypt. A civilization whose mysteries, symbols, and sacred sites have called to the human soul for thousands of years. There is something about this land that strips away the noise and brings you back to remembering.

We stand to bridge ancient wisdom to modern society.

Our Vision

Our vision is to maintain an authentic, ethical space for self-exploration and healing, and to provide greater ease and comfort for participants — so they can carry more peace, wisdom, and sustainability back into the world.

We aim to keep our experiences rich in cultural history, knowledge, and healing, while continuously expanding into new destinations and dimensions of this work. Since 2022, we have guided seven sacred journeys — and we are just beginning.

The Modern Ashram represents the frequency of wholeness, completion, and expansion…

We believe we can weave together a global community united by a shared understanding of how to co-create a new reality and consciousness for thriving in today's world.

While Egypt remains the heart of our work, our vision extends to the many other sacred geographies the world still holds. Future journeys will reach into the world's most potent sites — places that have called to seekers for millennia — each chosen for its cultural depth, its energetic resonance, and its capacity to facilitate the same return to wholeness our work has always pointed toward.

Our story

Our founder Fouad's story

The Modern Ashram's
journey of evolution

The Modern Ashram began in my apartment in Barcelona in 2019. After feeling swept up by the pace of the corporate world and the expectations of modern society, I felt a deep calling to create spaces that brought people together in a more conscious, intentional way.

Drawing from my passion for ancient plant and earth medicines, healing modalities, and spiritual practices, I wanted to simplify access to these tools for the modern individual.

From this vision, The Modern Ashram was born — a bridge between contemporary life and sacred work, connecting modern individuals to ancient wisdom, intentional containers, and powerful locations around the world.

Born and raised in Cairo, Egypt, Fouad is a former corporate sales executive turned certified healer, with expertise in Energy Medicine, Breathwork, Meditation, and toad medicine facilitation. His spiritual journey began during his corporate career, following a period of deep depression that led him to seek answers beyond conventional approaches. Rather than pursuing traditional treatments, Fouad turned inward, immersing himself in meditation, the practice of stillness, and holistic living under the guidance of Eastern teachers.

In 2016, his growing curiosity about alternative healing modalities led him to a transformative plant medicine journey in Peru, where he experienced profound shifts that realigned him with his deeper purpose. This pivotal experience inspired him to further his studies in holistic practices and energy work, complemented by immersive training in toad medicine in Spain and Mexico.

Over the past six years, he has supported over a thousand individuals from around the world. He is the founder of The Modern Ashram, through which he continues to curate and co-create intentional healing spaces — currently focused in Egypt — designed to support transformation, self-discovery, and expanded awareness.
