The Invitation

The Journey

The Nile Alchemy Journey is an invitation to experience Egypt not only through its extraordinary history, but through your own direct experience. Exploring our inner landscapes through various modalities that align with the wisdom of the temples we are visiting each day.

For The Explorers. For The Seekers. For The Curious Travellers. The Nile Alchemy Journey unfolds in three distinct phases.

01

The Call

Opening with a private meditation inside the King's Chamber to begin our inner alchemical process. A doorway into the journey ahead.

02

The Alchemy

A journey aboard a private luxury Dahabiya, traveling between the great temples of Egypt while weaving together breathwork, flow-dance meditation, sufi whirling, and sacred ceremony.

03

The Integration

A spacious landing. Time to reflect, absorb and integrate all that the journey has revealed, while exploring some of Egypt's most extraordinary sacred sites.

Hosted By

Meet your facilitators

Three experienced guides hold space with wisdom, embodiment and deep knowledge of Egypt's sacred sites.

Lindsay Jay

Lindsay Jay is the founder of Flow Dance Meditation and co-founder of YogaFit Retreats. She specializes in creating spaces to move, heal, and transform through movement, meditation, and embodied awareness.

Throughout this journey, Lindsay will guide Flow Dance Meditation, Sufi whirling, guided meditations and integration circles, offering space to slow down, listen deeply and integrate the magic of Egypt. From the Great Pyramid to the banks of the Nile, each practice is an invitation to experience this ancient land not only through seeing, but through feeling.

Fouad Sabet(The Modern Ashram)

Fouad Sabet is the founder of the Modern Ashram. His work is titled "Bridging Ancient Wisdom with the Modern World." He has helped more than a thousand people return to themselves, discover their true essence, and heal trauma and inner blockages. He will be guiding people in his own land, facilitating ceremony and breathwork.

He started his journey by learning about meditation, the art of stillness, and holistic living from Eastern Gurus and teachers. His passion and goal are to help humanity thrive as individuals living in an increasingly busy and stressful world. Fouad's work involves realigning individuals through their unique energetic signature and purpose.

Cat Tettero

Cat creates spaces for internal connection, where the wisdom of your body and all parts of our experience can be welcomed into awareness. Her work is rooted in the understanding that our relationship with our inner world becomes the foundation from which we meet ourselves, others, and life.

Through presence, breath, touch, and safe expression, she supports people in reconnecting with their felt sense of agency — the capacity to respond to life with greater awareness, freedom, and aliveness.

Ramy, the founder of Sukoon Studios, has been guiding people on journeys in Egypt for 20 years. He has recently shifted his energy and direction towards a more holistic approach to inner journeys — guiding people through their own inner journeys while sharing his roots and the wisdom of the Egyptian land and culture.
